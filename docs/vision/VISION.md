# Vision

## Long-term company

Build an AI-native finance department for U.S. mid-market specialty contractors.

The customer retains the CFO / senior finance leader.

Our company eventually operates much of the finance function below that person using:
- AI agents for repetitive analysis, reconciliation, investigation, follow-up and preparation
- India-based project accountants / finance operators for exception resolution and review
- controller-level humans for material judgment and final review
- integrations into existing ERP, project-management, payroll, banking and document systems

We want to sell completed outcomes and SLAs, not bodies or billable hours.

## Why specialty contractors

Their financial truth depends on both accounting and operating context:
- job costs
- labor productivity
- commitments
- long-lead material/equipment
- project progress
- change orders
- SOV and billing
- retainage
- PM forecasts
- close/cutoff decisions

Generic bookkeeping automation does not by itself resolve these cross-system operating questions.

## Expansion path

V0:
1. Project Forecast & WIP Close
2. AP & Cost Control
3. Billing & Change Order Readiness

Later:
4. AP processing / job-cost coding
5. AR / retainage / collections
6. payroll/labor reconciliation
7. GL reconciliations
8. full month-end close
9. multi-entity reporting
10. cash forecasting
11. management reporting / FP&A

Long term:
Run most recurring accounting and finance operations beneath the CFO.
